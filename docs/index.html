📖 How to Play GradQuest @ Yale

🎯 Goal: Publish 3 journal papers, complete your thesis, and defend!

📱 Mobile Navigation (4 Tabs):

  • 📊 Status: Your stats (date, morale, advisor, publications) + event messages
  • 🎯 Actions: All the things you can do each month
  • 🔬 Lab: Research pipeline, status effects, graduation progress
  • ⏩ Next: Advance to the next month

📊 Status Tab Explained:

  • 📅 Date: Current month/year and credits earned
  • 📰 Publications: Papers published (J=Journal, C=Conference)
  • 💪 Morale: Your mental health (drops monthly, recover with breaks)
  • 👨‍🏫 Advisor: Relationship with your advisor (affects outcomes)
  • 🤝 Network: Professional connections (unlocks bonuses at 40/60/80)
  • 📚 Quals Prep: Study level for qualifying exam (need 3+ to pass)
  • 🎯 Alignment: How well your research matches advisor's goals

🔬 Research Pipeline:

📚 Read Papers → 💡 Get Idea → 🔬 Develop Findings → 🎯 Key Discovery → 📊 Make Figures (×3) → 📝 Submit Paper

📋 Common Actions Explained:

  • 📚 Read Papers: Search for new research ideas (40% chance of idea)
  • 💡 Work on Idea: Develop idea into preliminary findings
  • 🔬 Develop Findings: Turn findings into a key discovery
  • 📊 Make Figures: Create figures for your paper (need 3)
  • 📝 Draft Paper: Write and submit to journal/conference
  • ✍️ Write Thesis: Work on your dissertation (~12% progress each time)
  • 🎓 Pitch Session: Meet with advisor (learn preferences, gain alignment)
  • 📚 Study for Quals: Prepare for qualifying exam
  • ☕ Take Break: Rest to recover morale (+15)

📰 Publications:

  • Journal Paper: 8-12 months review, counts toward graduation
  • Conference Paper: 4 months, builds network (+15)

⚠️ Key Events:

  • Qual Exam: September Year 2 (prep level 3+ to pass!)
  • Teaching Load: When funding runs out (-50% speed)
  • Emergency Grant: Escape teaching load (appears when funding low)

🏆 Graduation Requirements:

  • ✅ Pass qualifying exam
  • ✅ Publish 3+ journal papers
  • ✅ Complete thesis (100%)
  • ✅ Defend (final action!)
