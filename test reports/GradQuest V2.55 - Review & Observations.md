**Date:** January 31, 2026  
**Game Version:** V2.55  
**URL:** [https://xy-vince.github.io/GradQuest/](https://xy-vince.github.io/GradQuest/)

---

## 1. Major Observations in V2.55

Version 2.55 introduces several significant refinements to the core loop and user interface, particularly focusing on clarity and thematic immersion.

### 📊 UI & UX Enhancements
*   **Focused Mode:** The header now explicitly states "Focused Mode: Core Progression," signaling a more streamlined experience.
*   **Status Cards Refinement:** The top status bar has been reorganized. Key metrics like **Stress**, **Buffs**, **Tension**, and **Quals Prep** now have dedicated cards, making them much easier to track than in previous versions.
*   **Research Pipeline Visualization:** A new visual breadcrumb trail (`Ideas → Findings → Discovery → Figures`) has been added above the action buttons. This provides excellent immediate feedback on where the player stands in the publication cycle.
*   **Thematic Event Messaging:** The "What Happened" log feels more dynamic, with flavor text like "Reviewer 2 would hate this figure" adding a humorous yet realistic touch to the academic simulation.

### ⚙️ Gameplay Mechanics
*   **Quals Window:** The game now provides a clear "QUALS WINDOW OPEN!" warning, emphasizing the importance of the Year 2 milestone.
*   **Labmate Presence:** Events like "Marcus published!" provide a sense of a living lab environment, even if direct interaction is still limited.
*   **Field Specializations:** The core differences between Experimentalist, Theoretician, and Computational remain distinct, with V2.55 feeling more balanced in terms of risk/reward for each path.

---

## 2. Suggestions for Future Improvements

While V2.55 is highly polished, the following areas could be enhanced in future versions:

### 📱 Mobile-First Optimization
*   **Action Button Grouping:** As the action list grows (especially with seasonal conference actions), the grid can still feel slightly overwhelming. Implementing a "Research / Personal / Admin" tab system within the Actions section would improve tap accuracy on small screens.
*   **Sticky Progress:** Consider making the "Graduation Progress" bar sticky at the bottom of the screen so players can always see their ultimate goal without scrolling past the research pipeline.

### 🎮 Gameplay Depth
*   **Active Labmate Interaction:** Building on the "Marcus published" event, allow players to "Collaborate" with labmates. This could trade some of your own progress for a boost in Network or a reduction in Stress.
*   **Advisor Personalities:** Introduce different advisor "archetypes" (e.g., The Micro-manager, The Ghost, The Supportive Mentor) that change the frequency and impact of "Pitch Session" outcomes.
*   **Grant Writing Mini-game:** Instead of a single button click, make grant writing a multi-stage process similar to the research pipeline, with higher credit rewards for successful completion.

### 🎨 Visual & Narrative
*   **Dynamic Backgrounds:** Subtle visual shifts (e.g., a coffee cup appearing during "Take a Break" or a stack of papers growing on the desk) would add a layer of "juice" to the text-based interface.
*   **Career Paths:** Upon graduation, provide a short narrative summary of the player's career based on their stats (e.g., "Became a Tenure-Track Professor" vs. "Industry Data Scientist").

---

## 3. Final Verdict
**GradQuest V2.55 is the most stable and readable version yet.** The new status cards and research pipeline visualization significantly lower the barrier to entry for new players while maintaining the deep, often stressful, strategic core that makes the simulation so resonant.
