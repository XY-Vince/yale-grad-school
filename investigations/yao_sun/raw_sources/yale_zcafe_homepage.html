The AI in Social Science Research Methods Conference will return to Yale in 2027!

The conference brings together scholars across the social sciences, statistics, and data science to discuss how AI is changing the way we conduct social science research—from measurement and data collection to causal inference, theory building, scientific discovery, and the future of research practice.
