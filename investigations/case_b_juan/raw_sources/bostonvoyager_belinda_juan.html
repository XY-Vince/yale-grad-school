Meet Belinda Juan of Bliss Spa & Salon

Local Stories
Share
Tweet
Pin

Today we’d like to introduce you to Belinda Juan.

Belinda, can you briefly walk us through your story – how you started and how you got to where you are today.
I started my journey at Whittier Regional Vocational High school in the Cosmetology program (2001). After graduating I attended SNHU as a full-time student while working at the salon, I currently own to pursue my business degree (2005).

In 2008 I was presented with the opportunity to purchase Bliss (at the time it was named Paradise). On June 25th we celebrated our ten year anniversary.

What was the biggest challenge or the most difficult time for the salon?
When I took over the salon/spa in 2008 that is when we had the recession, and it was very difficult to stay afloat.

So let’s switch gears a bit and go into the Bliss Spa & Salon story. Tell us more about the salon.
Bliss Spa & Salon is a hair salon providing everything from cuts, & color services to smoothing (keratin) and curling services (American wave). Plus we offer Spa services as well facials, massages, lash tinting/lifting extensions we also offer natural nail services manicures, pedicures & gel manicures.

I am the owner of Bliss and also work on the floor providing hair services four days a week. All staff is kept up to date with in-house or travel education on any new product or service entering Bliss.

What makes me proud as a company is my staff they are amazing, and without them, there would be no Bliss. They work as a team, and we can share ideas and work together.

What sets us apart is we try to stay ahead of the curve with services and make our guests feel special. This September we are launching our CBD oil massage. People with arthritis, broken bones, sports injuries, and overworked muscles from working out, neurological disorders, and those who want to relax or relieve anxiety can benefit from CBD oil treatments.

How do you think the industry will change over the next decade?
I still see fashion shades taking over, and natural curl is huge!

Pricing:

  • Haircuts $45-$65
  • color services $45-$122
