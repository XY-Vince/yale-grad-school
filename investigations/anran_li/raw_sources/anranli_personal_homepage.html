Dr. Anran Li

Pre-tenure Professor at University of Science and Technology of China

About me: Dr. Anran Li is currently a Pre-Tenure Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). Prior to joining USTC, Dr. Anran Li was a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biomedical Informatics & Data Science, School of Medicine at Yale University (Yale University School of Medicine), where I worked with Prof. Qingyu Chen and also closely collaborated with Prof. Hua Xu, Prof. Lucila Ohno-Machado (MD, MBA, PhD), and Prof. Hyunghoon Cho. Before that, Dr. Anran Li was a Research Fellow in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Yang Liu, Prof. Han Yu and Prof. Luu Anh Tuan. Dr. Anran Li received Ph.D degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at USTC (University of Science and Technology of China), advised by Prof. Xiangyang Li and Prof. Lan Zhang. My research interests include trustworthy AI, large language models (LLMs), federated learning and graph learning.
